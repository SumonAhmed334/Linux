# Bangladesh Bank Cybersecurity Framework 2026 — Audit Control Matrix

Source: Bangladesh Bank, BRPD Circular No. 02 dated 29 March 2026, Cybersecurity Framework Version 1.0 (2026).

**Important:** P1/P2/P3 are an audit risk-based prioritization created for implementation sequencing; they are not Bangladesh Bank's prescribed priority labels. The circular requires compliance by **31 December 2026**.

## Priority model

- **P1 – Critical:** direct compromise prevention/detection, privileged access, data protection, incident containment/recovery, or regulatory response.
- **P2 – High:** material governance, risk, configuration, supplier, monitoring, or operational controls supporting primary security outcomes.
- **P3 – Medium:** important baseline/process controls with comparatively lower immediate impact; still required for compliance.

Without a Cybersecurity program, the Organization cannot defend itself against data breach campaigns, which makes it an irresistible target for cybercriminals. Every organization needs to manage Cybersecurity risk as a part of doing business, whether it is in industry, government or academia. It is critical to their resilience and to our nation‘s economic security. This document represents an initial, high-level summary of the Cybersecurity responses. This Framework for improving Critical Cybersecurity Infrastructure is released in March 2026 after extensive public engagement and collaboration from different banks and Financial Institutions of the country. This framework was intended to be a living document that is refined, improved, and evolves over time to keep pace with technology and threat trends, integrate lessons learned, and move best practice to common practice.
